What's stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and sign-in session in your browser's local storage, and keeps a copy of the game files so it can work offline. This is needed for the game to work, so we don't ask for consent for it. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings (you'll lose any progress that isn't linked to an email).

Who helps us run the game

These companies process data for us, only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in service. Your account, scores and saved progress are stored on its servers in London (UK).
  • Resend: sends the sign-in code emails. It receives your email address and the email content.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosts the game's files.
  • Cloudflare: runs our domain's DNS, which tells your browser where to find the game.

Some of these providers are based in, or may access data from, the United States. Where that happens, they protect it with safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

Who can see what

  • Other players can see your display name, scores and the number of exact answers on the leaderboards, and your name in Battles you join.
  • Anyone with your friend code can see your display name and your scores on their Friends leaderboards.
  • Your email address is never shown to other players.
  • When you share your results, the shared card shows your score pattern, not your name or email.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, email, scores and saved progress: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Battle rooms: deleted automatically, usually within a day.
  • Server logs: kept by our providers for a short period for security, typically days to weeks.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data (you can also download it in the game: Settings, then Export my data);
  • have it corrected (you can change your display name in Settings at any time);
  • have it deleted;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable format.

To use any of these, email privacy@playdialin.com from the address on your profile (or tell us your friend code if you haven't added an email). We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

DIAL IN is a general-audience game. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before adding your email. If you believe a child under 13 has given us their email without permission, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let players know in the game.
