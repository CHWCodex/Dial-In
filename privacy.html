Privacy notice

Last updated 21 November 2026

The short version: you can play DIAL IN without giving us anything personal. To save your account you pick a username and a 6-digit PIN: no email, no real name. If you buy DIAL IN+, the payment is handled by Stripe and we never see your card details. We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

Who we are

DIAL IN (playdialin.com) is run by Charlie Hilton-West and James Alden, trading together as Dial In, in the United Kingdom. We are the "controller" of your personal data under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), and we are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Questions or requests about your data: privacy@playdialin.com.

Moving away from email

Until 5 December 2026, players who joined with an email can sign in with it once more to move their account to a username and PIN. When someone moves across, we delete their email straight away. On 5 December 2026 we delete every email address still held, including unconfirmed ones, and email sign-in ends. Anyone who hasn't moved across stays signed in on the device they use and can save a username and PIN from there at any time.

What's stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and sign-in session in your browser's local storage, and keeps a copy of the game files so it can work offline. This is needed for the game to work, so we don't ask for consent for it. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings, or use Sign out in Settings (you'll lose any progress that isn't saved to an account).

Daily reminders

If you switch on the daily reminder, your browser gives us a notification address for your device. It's stored with your player ID and used only for the reminder. Notifications are delivered through your browser or phone maker's push service (such as Google or Apple), which sees only that address and the encrypted message. Turning the reminder off deletes the address straight away; we also delete addresses that stop working.

Who helps us run the game

These companies process data for us, only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in service. Your account, scores and saved progress are stored on its servers in London (UK).
  • Resend: sent the sign-in code emails for players who joined with an email. Until 5 December 2026 it may still send a code to a player moving their account across; after that we don't use it.
  • Stripe: takes payments for DIAL IN+ and any tips. Stripe collects your card details, email and billing information directly, and acts as its own controller for that data under its privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the game's files, runs our domain and protects the site from attacks.

Some of these providers are based in, or may access data from, the United States. Where that happens, they protect it with safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

Who can see what

  • Everyone who plays the daily puzzle appears on the public leaderboards, so other players can see your display name, scores, streak and number of exact answers, and a verified tick if you've saved an account. Your name also shows in Battles you join. Pick a nickname rather than your real name if you'd prefer.
  • Members of a league you join can see your display name, your scores and whether you’ve played today. Anyone with a league’s link can see its name and how many players it has.
  • Anyone with your friend code can see your display name and your scores on their Friends leaderboards.
  • If you buy DIAL IN+, a SUPPORTER badge appears next to your name on the leaderboards. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.
  • Your username and PIN are never shown to other players.
  • When you share your results, the shared card shows your score pattern, not your name.

How long we keep it

  • Saved accounts, scores and progress: until you delete your account (Settings, then Delete my account and data) or ask us to.
  • Guest profiles (never saved to an account): deleted automatically after 12 months without being played or opened.
  • Email addresses: deleted as soon as you move to a username and PIN, and in any case on 5 December 2026.
  • Scrambled connection tags: 48 hours. Codes for adding a device: 10 minutes.
  • Purchase records: six years, as UK tax rules require. If you delete your account, the record is kept without the link to you.
  • Battle rooms: deleted automatically, usually within a day.
  • Reminder notification addresses: until you turn reminders off, or the address stops working.
  • Server logs: kept by our providers for a short period for security, typically days to weeks.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data (download all of it in the game: Settings, then Export my data);
  • have it corrected (you can change your display name in Settings at any time);
  • have it deleted (Settings, then Delete my account and data; this happens straight away);
  • object to or restrict how we use it, including the connection tags used to keep the leaderboards fair;
  • receive it in a portable format (the export is a standard JSON file).

You can also email privacy@playdialin.com. Tell us your username or friend code so we can find your account (we never need your PIN). We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first at the address above and we'll look into it. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

DIAL IN is a general-audience game that children may play. It's built to need no personal details: no email, no real name, no location, no contacts, and no ads or tracking. Reminders are off unless you turn them on. Only your chosen display name appears to others. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before saving an account or buying DIAL IN+. A parent can delete a child's account at any time in Settings, or by emailing us.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let players know in the game.
