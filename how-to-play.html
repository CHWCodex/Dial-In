How to play DIAL IN

DIAL IN is a free daily number trivia game. Every day there are seven new questions, and every answer is a number: a year, a distance, a height, a record. Guess as close as you can, watch the dial, and see how many players you beat.

The daily puzzle

  • Seven questions a day, the same for everyone. A new puzzle starts at midnight UK time.
  • Type your answer on the keypad and tap Lock it in.
  • Watch the dial. The right answer sits at the top. Too low swings left, too high swings right. The closer the needle, the stronger the signal.
  • Big answers come with a hint. If the answer is 1,000 or more, you’ll see a rough range.
  • You have 20 seconds a question. When time runs out, whatever you’ve typed is locked in.

Each question is worth up to 1,000 points, so a perfect day scores 7,000 before any bonus.

Years and small numbers

Year questions score by how many years off you are: exact 1,000, within 1 year 900, 3 years 750, 5 years 600, 10 years 400, 25 years 200.

Small counts (answers of 12 or less) score by how many you’re off: exact 1,000, off by 1 scores 600, by 2 scores 400, by 3 scores 200.

Some answers that are naturally approximate, like “roughly how many species of penguin are there?”, accept a sensible range as exact. The explanation after each question tells you when that’s the case.

Dial Ball: the pinball bonus round

Finish the daily puzzle and you unlock Dial Ball, a pinball machine where the holes are dials. Your answers earn the balls:

An arrow at the bottom sweeps left and right. Tap Launch when it points where you want; the flippers and bumpers do the rest. Land in a dial to score its number: 250 for the jackpot at the top, 100, 50 or 25. Every bumper hit adds 5. Your Dial Ball points go on top of your daily score.

More ways to play

Unlimited

Keep answering questions for as long as you can with three hearts. An answer more than 20% off, or running out of time, costs a heart. Chase your best score and longest run.

Battle

Play live against two to four friends. One person creates a room and shares its four-digit code; everyone answers the same question against the clock, and the worst answer each round loses a life. The last player standing wins.

Leaderboards

Add your email to post your daily scores and see the Daily, All-time and Friends leaderboards. It’s one profile per email, so you appear once however many devices you play on, and your progress follows you. Swap friend codes in Settings to compare scores with friends.

Tips

  • When you’re unsure, anchor on something you know. A London bus is about 11 metres long; Everest is 8,849 metres high.
  • For years, think about what else was happening at the time: being within three years still scores 750.
  • Use the hint range on big numbers, and aim for the middle of it if you’re stuck.
  • Keep your streak going: a new puzzle is waiting every day at midnight UK time.

Questions

Is DIAL IN free?

Yes. The daily puzzle, Dial Ball, Unlimited and Battles are all free to play.

Do I need to download an app?

No. DIAL IN runs in any web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. You can add it to your home screen to open it like an app, and it works offline once loaded.

How many questions are there?

Over 1,150 hand-checked questions covering history, science, space, sport, geography, film, TV, music, food, money and more, with new ones added over time.

When does the next puzzle start?

Every day at midnight UK time.
