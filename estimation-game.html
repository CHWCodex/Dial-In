Tips for better estimates

  • Anchor on something you know. A double-decker bus is about 11 metres long, a football pitch about 100 metres, Mount Everest 8,849 metres high.
  • Break it down. Estimate a smaller piece you’re sure of, then multiply up. This is how scientists make “Fermi estimates”.
  • Think in orders of magnitude. Is it closer to 100, 1,000 or 10,000? Getting the size right earns most of the points.
  • For years, use landmarks. What else was happening at the time? Within three years still scores well.

Why estimation games are good for you

Estimating builds number sense: the feel for how big things are that helps with everything from budgeting to spotting a dodgy statistic. It’s also a great leveller. Nobody knows every answer, so a good guess can beat a know-it-all, which makes it ideal for families, classrooms and friendly rivalries.
