How to play

  1. Every answer is a number. Tap it in on the keypad and hit Lock it in.
  2. Watch the dial. The answer sits at the top. Too low swings left, too high swings right. The closer the needle, the stronger the signal.
  3. Big answers come with a hint. If the answer is 1,000 or more, you’ll see a rough range.
  4. 20 seconds a question. When time runs out, whatever you’ve typed is locked in.
  5. Daily: 7 questions, the same for everyone, new at midnight UK time. Finish to see how many players you beat.
  6. Unlimited: keep going as long as you can with 3 hearts. More than 20% off loses a heart.
  7. Battle: 2–4 friends in a room. Worst answer each round loses a life.
