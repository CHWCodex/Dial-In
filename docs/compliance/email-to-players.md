# Email to the players who signed up with an email

Send this once, from hello@playdialin.com, to each player who still has an email on their profile. Put the addresses in **BCC**, never To or CC, so nobody sees anyone else's email. Get the list in Supabase: Authentication → Users, filtered to emails that don't end in @accounts.playdialin.com. Don't add any marketing: this is a service message about their account.

---

**Subject:** DIAL IN: we're dropping email sign-in on 5 December

Hi,

You joined DIAL IN with this email address. We're making DIAL IN simpler and more private: from now on, accounts use a username and a 6-digit PIN instead of an email.

**What you need to do (takes 30 seconds), before 5 December 2026:**
1. Open playdialin.com on the phone or computer you usually play on.
2. Tap the banner at the top ("Email sign-in ends on 5 December"), or go to Settings.
3. Pick a username and a PIN, and save the recovery code it shows you.

Everything comes with you: your scores, streak, leagues, friends and DIAL IN+ if you bought it. As soon as you've done it, we delete your email address.

**If you don't do it by 5 December,** we'll delete your email anyway and you'll stay signed in on the device you use. You can save a username and PIN from there whenever you like. Until you do, you won't get the verified tick next to your name on the leaderboards.

Playing on a different device to the one you joined on? Until 5 December you can sign in there with your email one last time (Settings, then "Signed up with email before? Move it across").

Any questions, just reply to this email.

Thanks for playing,
Charlie and James
DIAL IN
