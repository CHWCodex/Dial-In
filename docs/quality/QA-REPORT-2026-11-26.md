## 2. Safeguards built

| Area | What exists now |
|---|---|
| **Same-fact detection** (`scripts/question-audit.mjs`) | Catches the same fact in different clothes:<br>• wording changes (normalised text, word fingerprints, similarity scores);<br>• the same subject plus the same answer, including unit conversions (m/ft, km/mi, kg/lb, °C/°F and more);<br>• Fact Check claims flipped from true to false;<br>• cross-mode overlaps.<br>Every flag needs a recorded human decision. |
| **Reviewed decisions** (`content/`) | Fact groups, quarantine, rewording log, 856 reviewed "different fact" pairs and time-sensitive questions judged stable. |
| **Schedule** (`scripts/build-content.mjs`) | From 28 Nov, a reviewed list instead of the rolling shuffle:<br>• a fact is used at most once, ever;<br>• related questions are kept 14 days apart;<br>• Fact Check stays clear of that day's number topics (±3 days);<br>• the schedule stops rather than repeat. |
| **Usage record** | `content/question-usage.json`: for each question, its fact, status, publication dates, scheduled date, source and verification.<br>Database: `questions.fact_id/status/reviewed_on`, plus the `question_usage` view, which players can't read. |
| **Reuse policy** | Defined per mode in `docs/content/RUNBOOK.md`:<br>• **Daily:** never reuse.<br>• **Archive:** the intentional exception.<br>• **Unlimited:** no repeats within a cycle, recent questions skipped across runs.<br>• **Battles:** no fact twice per room, across rematches.<br>• **Unlimited and Battles:** never anything from the daily window (3 days back, 30 ahead). |
| **Publication guard** (database trigger) | • Published days can't be edited or deleted.<br>• No fact can be reused, and quarantined or unknown questions are refused.<br>• Concurrent schedulers are serialised.<br>• A controlled correction procedure exists for genuine errors. |
| **Validation gate** (`scripts/validate-questions.mjs`) | Checks fields, sources, British spelling, giveaways, time-sensitive wording, duplicates, history, schedule rules and stock. Runs on every PR and push (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) and blocks the release. |
| **Monitoring** (nightly) | • Records each published day.<br>• Refreshes the usage record.<br>• Fails the run (GitHub emails the owner) on a changed published day, a reused fact, or fewer than 14 days of daily puzzles or 7 days of Fact Check left.<br>• Warns at 30 days. |
| **Graceful failure** | If a day has no reviewed puzzle, players get a holding screen ("Today's puzzle isn't ready yet…") with routes to Unlimited and past puzzles. Never a repeat, never a crash. |

**Tested and confirmed correct (no change needed):**
- the timer can't be extended by refreshing;
- 6 simultaneous taps record one answer;
- the timer and an answer arriving together give one result;

## 5. Remaining risks and limits

1. **Future answers ship to the browser.** The whole bank and schedule are in `index.html`, as before, so a determined player can read upcoming answers or look them up while the timer runs. The public API exposes nothing (row-level security). Fixing this means serving questions from the server: an architectural change that needs your approval, and it would also change offline play.
2. **Timing is checked in the browser only.** Moving the phone's clock changes only local timing; scores are calculated on the server from the guesses.
3. **Facts are checked editorially, not against their sources.**
4. **Fact Check runs out on 30 Dec** without the draft approval or new statements. The nightly check fails from 23 Dec.
5. **c25 repeats on 27 Nov** unless the deploy happens before midnight tonight.
6. **Not tested:**
   - real iOS/Android devices, Safari, Firefox, Edge, and TikTok/Instagram in-app browsers (Chromium emulation only);
   - real Stripe test-mode checkout;
   - push notifications;
   - load testing (only concurrency tests on staging and locally).
7. **Battles trust the host's app.** A modified app could cheat in a private room; there's no global leaderboard effect.
8. **Back button.** The app doesn't use browser history, so Back leaves the game, the same as switching apps (the timer keeps running by design).
9. **Dial Ball.** Balls in flight when the page is refreshed are lost. This is deliberate, so the round can't be re-rolled.
